## Draft angle

**Process lesson:** When combining N separate pages into one continuous page, keep old routes intact until every section is verified on the new page. This prevents broken links and allows gradual migration.

**Positioning angle:** The distinction between "page 1, page 2, etc." as sections within a project vs. separate routes is a common point of confusion in portfolio site architecture. Clear terminology prevents UX issues.
